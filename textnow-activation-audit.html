why now
Three reasons activation is the lever worth pulling this quarter.

A free-first model lives or dies on activation

TextNow monetizes through ads and upgrades, so revenue is a direct function of how many users reach a real, recurring habit. Three things make this the moment to get it right.

01

A new product surface to onboard

eSIM, flexible data plans, and the upgraded nationwide network each introduce fresh first-run flows. Every new path is a new place for users to stall before they feel the value.

02

Ad revenue compounds on retention

With an ad-supported free tier, a user who returns daily is worth multiples of one who churns in week one. Activation is the cheapest growth you have already paid to acquire.

03

You are consolidating lifecycle

Your mandate spans brand, growth, and lifecycle. An outside-in audit gives you a fast, unbiased baseline of where the program stands before you commit headcount and roadmap.

03
the thesis
Activation is not one moment. It is four distinct decisions.

Four stages decide whether a user stays

We map your funnel against the four points where free-telecom users choose to stay or go. Most teams over-invest in stage 01 and under-invest in 02 and 03.

STAGE 01

Setup

Install to a working number. Friction, permissions, and verification dropout before any value is felt.

STAGE 02

First value

The first sent text or completed call. The strongest single predictor of week-one retention.

STAGE 03

Habit

Repeat use across days. Where lifecycle messaging and reasons-to-return do the heavy lifting.

STAGE 04

Commitment

Keeping the number, adding a SIM, or upgrading. The bridge from free habit to paid intent.
