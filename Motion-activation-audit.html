01 · Why this, why now

Your product wins on the "aha." Activation is where you lose people before they reach it.

Motion's promise is unusually clear: connect an ad account and instantly see what's working, why, and what to make next. That's a real aha moment, most analytics tools never earn one. But the value sits behind a wall: a trial user has to authenticate a Meta ad account over OAuth, wait for a sync, then know where to look first.

Every step between "Start for free" and "oh, that's my winning pattern" is an activation problem. And activation is the seam where product and marketing meet: positioning, empty states, first-session copy, lifecycle nudges. You've sat on both sides of that seam, which is exactly why this teardown is built for you.

The thesis is simple: Motion's biggest activation lever isn't a new feature. It's compressing time-to-first-insight and making the path to it impossible to miss.

"A self-serve trial converts on one thing: how fast a stranger reaches the moment your product is obviously worth paying for."

03 · Five hypotheses to pressure-test

Where trial momentum likely leaks, and what each one costs.

Read these like a Motion report: each finding flagged the way Motion flags creative, scaling, fatiguing, or declining. They're testable hypotheses from an outside-in read, not verdicts.

FINDING H-01▼ Declining risk

The aha is gated behind an OAuth wall a trial user may not own.

"Connect a Meta ad account" assumes the person trying Motion has admin access. Strategists and junior buyers often don't, so they stall at the highest-intent moment. Is there a path to value before the connection, a sample workspace or guided demo data?

▲ Moves: connect-rate · trial→activated
FINDING H-02► Fatiguing

Sync + tagging creates dead air between effort and payoff.

The flow tells users to "explore Inspo while data loads." Helpful, but the gap between connecting and seeing their own winning patterns is where trials go cold. How is that wait choreographed, and is a "first insight" moment engineered into it?

▲ Moves: time-to-first-insight · D1 return
FINDING H-03► Fatiguing

"First report built" ≠ "first insight understood."

Motion builds the first report automatically, but does a first-timer know which number means they're winning? Interpretation is Motion's edge. The activation question is whether the first state teaches the read or just shows the chart.

▲ Moves: feature adoption · perceived value
FINDING H-04▲ Watch

The agency aha needs more than one connected account.

ICP-2 (agencies) feels the magic across many brands, cross-account reporting and client snapshots. But the trial leads with a single connection. Does the agency persona get a distinct activation path, or the same one as a solo brand?

▲ Moves: agency activation · seat expansion
FINDING H-05▲ Watch

The self-serve / "book a demo" fork may strand mid-market.

Under $25k spend, explore alone; over $25k, book a demo. Clean on paper, but mid-market trial users who could self-activate may get routed to sales (slower) or left without enough hand-holding. Where's the assisted-PLG middle path?

▲ Moves: trial→paid by segment · sales load
